Terms of Service

Payment
  • A 50% deposit is required upfront before I begin your commission.
  • The remaining 50% must be paid once the commission is completed.
  • The final, high-resolution artwork will be sent to you immediately after the remaining balance has been paid.
Updates & Changes
  • I will provide updates and progress previews throughout the commission process.
  • Minor changes can be requested during the process. However, large or significant changes requested late in the process, particularly during the final line-art or rendering stages, may incur an additional fee.
Refunds
  • Full refunds are available if I have not yet started working on your commission.
  • If I am already more than halfway through the commission, a partial refund may be provided, depending on the amount of work completed.
  • Once the artwork is near completion, refunds may no longer be available.
Time & Patience
  • Please keep in mind that art takes time! Commission completion times may vary depending on the complexity of the artwork and my current workload.
  • I appreciate your patience and will work to complete your commission as soon as possible while maintaining the quality of the artwork.
Usage & Permissions
  • You are welcome to use the commissioned artwork as a profile picture (PFP) and post it on social media or other online platforms.
